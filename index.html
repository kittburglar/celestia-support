Why don't I see AI readings or Ask the Stars?

AI-written readings, compatibility readings and Ask the Stars use Apple Intelligence, which runs on iPhone 15 Pro and newer. Turn it on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones, Celestia shows its built-in daily readings, and personality and compatibility work on every iPhone.

How do I change my birthdate?

Tap the moon button in the top-right corner of the main screen, pick your birthdate and tap Save.

How is compatibility worked out?

Celestia follows traditional astrology: the angle between two signs on the zodiac wheel, plus how their elements (fire, earth, air and water) combine. Love and friendship are scored separately.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your birthdate and questions never leave your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
