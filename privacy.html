Celestia Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Celestia ("the app") is built by Kittiphong Xayasane. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores

Celestia asks for your birthdate so it can work out your zodiac sign. The birthdate is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Questions you ask

Daily readings, compatibility readings and answers to "Ask the Stars" questions are written by Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence). Your questions are processed entirely on your iPhone and are never sent to us or stored by the app.

What the app does not do

Celestia does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

You can change your birthdate at any time from the moon button in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
